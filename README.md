# Case Study - Financial Dashboard Creation
#### 1. Dashboard Overview
This Dashboard analyzes company financial performance including revenue, budget variance, profitability, receivables aging and cash flow.

#### Objective 

You are working as a Data Analyst at The Finance Group. The client has requested a monthlylevel interactive dashboard in Power BI to monitor the company's financial health and key KPIs. Your task is to design and deliver a dashboard that allows filtering by date range and other key business metrics. 
#### Data:
- Monthly P&L Data (Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Opex, EBITDA) 
- Cash Flow Data (Inflows, Outflows, Net Cash) 
- Sales Data (Product/Service, Region, Month) 
- Receivables & Payables Aging 
- Budget vs Actuals

#### 2.Overall Financial Performance (Top KPIs)

- Total Budget: 31M

- Total Revenue: 24M

- Gross Margin: 46% (Strong profitability)

- EBITDA: 25% (Healthy operating performance)

- Net Cash Flow: 688K (Positive but relatively low vs revenue)

#### Insight:

Revenue is profitable (good margins), but actual revenue (24M) is significantly lower than budget (31M) → indicating underperformance vs target.

#### 2. Revenue & Gross Profit Trend (Monthly)

- Revenue fluctuates across months.

- Strong months: June, September, November

- Weak month: October (sharp drop)

#### Insight:

- October shows a noticeable dip in both revenue and gross profit → possible:

- Seasonal demand drop

- Supply issue

- Sales slowdown

- Business should investigate October performance.

#### 3. Budget vs Revenue Variance

- Revenue is consistently below budget.

- Budget variance % is negative across most months.

- Largest negative variance seen around March–April and October.

#### Insight:

The company is not meeting budget targets consistently.
Possible reasons:

- Overestimated forecasting

- Weak sales execution

- Pricing strategy issue

- Need tighter forecasting & sales strategy alignment.

#### 4. Receivables Aging (Working Capital Insight)

- Average aging ranges from 24 to 34 days

- Highest: May & November (34 days)

- Lowest: February (24 days)

#### Insight:

- Receivables aging above 30 days in multiple months → cash collection delay.

- This may explain why:
Net Cash Flow (688K) is relatively low despite good margins.

- Improve credit control & follow-ups.

#### 5. Revenue by Product/Service

Top Performers:

- Product C (4.9M)

- Service X (4.8M)

- Product B (4.7M)

- Lowest:

Product A (4.5M)

#### Insight:

- Revenue distribution is balanced, but:

- Product C & Service X drive the business.

- Product A underperforms → may need marketing push or pricing review.

#### 6. Cash Flow Waterfall

- Cash Inflows: 3M

- Cash Outflows: -2M

- Net Cash Flow: 1M (visual shows positive flow)

#### Insight:
- Business is cash-positive but margin between inflow & outflow is narrow.

- Any revenue slowdown or receivable delay can affect liquidity quickly.
